FAQ

A few common questions.

Does POPPAD replace my staff?

No. It handles routine reception information and self-service journeys while keeping a clear route to a real person when needed.

Is there a monthly POPPAD software fee?

The standard packages are designed without a compulsory monthly POPPAD software fee. External services remain separate.

Can I use my existing tablet?

Yes, subject to compatibility. POPPAD Digital is intended for businesses with suitable existing hardware.

Can customers book or pay through it?

POPPAD can guide customers to your existing booking, joining or payment pages. More complex live integrations can be scoped separately.

Can we update information later?

Yes. Future updates can be provided as separately charged changes after the initial included amendment/support period.
